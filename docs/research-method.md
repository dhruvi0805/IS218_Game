# Research methods and documentation

## Research Direction

The research will focus on:

- Game-based learning
- Educational mini-games
- Retrieval and repeated practice
- Visual representations of progress
- Gamification and motivation
- Cognitive skill activities
- Interactive learning environments
- Feedback and difficulty progression

The research site will present original sources with dates checked and explain how each source influenced the project.

Research claims will be separated from my interpretations and assumptions.

### Example structure

**Fact:** A research source reports a finding about a learning strategy.

**Interpretation:** This could be useful for a game because the player can repeatedly interact with the material.

**Assumption:** Players will be more willing to practice when the activity is represented as a short game.

**Design decision:** NeuroNet will use short activities rather than requiring a long continuous lesson.

**Experiment:** Test whether users understand the brain/network interface and whether they want to continue to another activity.

## AI Assistance

AI tools may be used during:

- Brainstorming
- Research-question development
- Architecture planning
- Programming assistance
- Debugging
- Reviewing possible game mechanics
- Generating initial ideas for mini-games

AI-generated information will not automatically be treated as factual.

For research claims, original sources will be opened and checked. Claims will be labeled based on whether they are supported by the original source.

For programming assistance, generated code will be tested locally and reviewed before being incorporated into the project.

The research site will document meaningful AI assistance and explain how claims or suggestions were verified.

---


## Verification Labels

Research and development notes will use the following labels:

### Planned

Something I intend to test or implement.

Example:

> Planned: Test whether users understand the brain network without an explanation.

### Attempted

Something I have tried but have not yet confirmed through sufficient testing.

Example:

> Attempted: Connect the memory mini-game's score to the visual progress node.

### Observed

Something that actually occurred during testing.

Example:

> Observed: A reviewer correctly identified the meaning of the completed node without additional explanation.

This prevents assumptions from being presented as established results.
