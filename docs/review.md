# Peer review

**Status: Planned**

1. Ask a classmate to open the original W3C source and point to the guidance behind the status labels.
2. Ask them to explain why the experiment compares a network and a list.
3. Ask them to identify one assumption or confusing part.

Take a turn reviewing their work as well.

Record specific feedback in a GitHub issue. Include what the reviewer said or did, the decision to implement, investigate, defer, or decline, and the reason. For accepted changes, link the edit, verification, and reviewer retry. Close resolved issues and leave a next step on unfinished issues.
